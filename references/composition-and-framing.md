# Composition & Camera Framing Blueprints (v2.0 — 10 Core Motifs)

Distilled from **167 curated Eastern classical cinema & environment references**. Apply these spatial laws equally to **3D film shots** and **game level-art sightlines (Vista Design)**.

---

## Part 1: The 10 Signature Framing Blueprints

### Blueprint A: Overhanging Botanical Canopy (枝叶穹顶压框)
- **Visual Ratio**: Upper & side `50%–70%` filled with dense, multi-layered overhanging foliage (crimson maples, golden ginkgo, drooping willow curtains, weeping cherry blossoms); lower `20%–35%` opens onto dark jade water with a tiny lone boat (`< 6%` screen width) or mossy stone steps buried in fallen leaves.
- **Anchor**: Heavy dark gnarled tree trunk (`#1c130a`) entering diagonally from a frame corner — never a thin centered pole.
- **Focal Length**: `50mm – 85mm`.

### Blueprint B: Extreme Dark-Frame-to-Bright-World (极暗框景 · 以暗框亮)
- **Visual Ratio**: Outer `45%–65%` is near-black (`lum < 0.10`) architectural silhouette — a dark corridor surrounding a circular moon gate, carved `冰裂纹` wooden lattice window, timber veranda columns looking toward a distant bridge, or dense dark cedar trunks framing a sunlit temple roof.
- **Focal Length**: `50mm – 105mm` with shallow physical DOF (`f/2.2 – f/4.0`).

### Blueprint C: Translucent Backlit Macro Poetry (逆光透射微距)
- **Hero Motifs**:
  1. **Under the Oil-Paper Umbrella (`油纸伞下仰拍`)**: Camera looks upward through radiating bamboo ribs and oiled paper while sunlight projects crisp crimson maple leaf silhouettes through the paper.
  2. **Dappled Wall Shadows (`粉墙/红墙树影`)**: Low raking sun (`12°–22°`) throws crisp swaying ginkgo/bamboo shadows across weathered lime plaster or cinnabar palace walls.
  3. **Backlit Eave + Ginkgo/Maple**: Dark tiled eave corner occupies one edge while translucent golden/crimson leaves glow fiercely against a restrained cool sky.

### Blueprint D: Liquid Painting Water & Ripple Micro-Story (油画水影与落花微漪)
- **Surface Rule**: Water is NEVER sterile chrome. Deep dark jade/obsidian (`#0c1e1e`) with dual-scale gentle swells that break crimson foliage or pavilion lights into painterly impasto strokes, or a single fallen blossom/leaf triggering concentric rain ripples (`同心涟漪`).

### Blueprint E: Three-Distance Ink-Wash Mist (三远烟雨层次)
- **Foreground (`1m – 4m`)**: Out-of-focus dark botanical screen (large matte lotus leaves, withered autumn lotus stems `残荷`, swaying reeds, hanging willow fronds).
- **Midground (`12m – 30m`)**: Mist-veiled upturned dark-tile eaves, arched bridge, or distant pagoda spire emerging softly from white-grey fog.
- **Background (`45m+`)**: Mountain ridges & forests dissolved `80%–95%` into cool slate-cyan mist.

### Blueprint F: Same-Camera State Awakening (同机位白模/四季唤醒)
- Lock exact camera & framing across two states (`暮云灰` monochrome clay/snow → beat-synced 1-frame cut into `琥珀金` autumn or `天水碧` mist; or spring sakura → summer jade → autumn gold → winter ink).

### Blueprint G: Black-Lacquer Mirror Interior & Shoji Slant (琉璃光院式漆案镜影与障子斜影) *(NEW in v2.0)*
- **Visual Ratio**: Pitch-dark traditional timber hall (`55%–75%` deep shadow `#170d06`) with circular windows (`源光庵式双圆窗`) or tall wooden mullions looking out at blazing autumn foliage, while a **mirror-polished black-lacquered table/floor (`Roughness 0.025–0.04`)** cleanly doubles the window vertically!
- **Variant**: Diagonal afternoon sunbeams slicing across white shoji paper walls (`障子纸斜影`) while narrow window slits reveal vertical scrolls of red maple forest.
- **Game/Film Advantage**: Extremely economical geometry (only dark room interior + window vista) yet produces instant jaw-dropping gallery photorealism in Cycles & UE5 Lumen.

### Blueprint H: Monumental Scale Contrast (巨山微屋 · 沧海一粟) *(NEW in v2.0)*
- **Visual Ratio**: `80%–90%` of frame occupied by a massive vertical sheer limestone cliff, dense dark conifer mountain wall, or brooding misty fjord; **`< 3%`** of frame holds a single tiny warm architectural anchor (a lone alpine chapel, pale hotel, red-roofed island cabin, or distant wooden boat).
- **Game Level-Art Application**: Ideal for **zone establishing vistas** and boss-approach horizons — creates immediate awe and sacred silence before the player descends into intimate garden interiors.

### Blueprint I: Translucent Gauze / Floating Calligraphy Veil & Soft Bokeh Screen (薄纱悬幔与柔焦前幕) *(NEW in v2.0)*
- **Visual Device**: Hang a sheer, wind-billowing white silk/gauze curtain (`薄纱飘幔`) or translucent calligraphy banner (`透光书法悬纱`) from a foreground dark eave — OR place lush green leaves directly `0.4m` in front of an `f/1.4` lens so out-of-focus bokeh creates a dreamy painterly veil framing golden ginkgo trees or misty pavilions behind.

### Blueprint J: 90° Top-Down Architectural Symmetry & Inverted Canopy Mirror (上帝视角天井与水镜倒枝) *(NEW in v2.0)*
- **Top-Down (`-90°` pitch)**: Moss-covered dark-tile roofs flanking a narrow sunken stone courtyard filled with blazing golden ginkgo crowns, OR a lone autumn island & red cabin floating in black abyssal water.
- **Inverted Canopy Reflection**: Point camera straight down at calm dark water reflecting an intricate black filigree of golden/crimson branches against blue sky, broken by crisp concentric raindrop rings; OR point straight up through gnarled dark night branches where glowing yellow ginkgo leaves scatter like stars against deep twilight cobalt.

## Part 2: Living Micro-Accents ("画眼 / 灵气点睛")

Never populate serene scenes with crowds or uncanny close-up faces. Instead, anchor loneliness with ONE quiet, small-scale pulse of life:
- **Sunbeam Animal**: A curled ginger cat basking in a warm pool of sunlight before a red temple door; two quiet deer stepping across mossy red maple leaves beneath a thatched hut.
- **Sky & Water Motion**: A small flock of dark birds wheeling between dark roof eaves; a line of tiny ducks or punts leaving faint silver wakes across misty water.
- **Veiled Silhouette (`< 8%` frame height)**: Back-view figure under an oil-paper umbrella, silhouette inside a giant backlit fog moon / moon gate (`水墨侠影`), or tiny solitary figure crossing a vast snowy courtyard.

## Part 3: Lens & Camera Discipline

| Scene Blueprint | Focal Length | Aperture (DOF) | Camera Motion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **C / G / I (Umbrella, Lacquer Mirror, Gauze Veil)** | `50mm – 105mm` | `f/1.8 – f/2.8` | Imperceptible drift / breeze animating cloth & leaves |
| **B (Moon Gate / Corridor / Shoji Shadows)** | `45mm – 75mm` | `f/3.2 – f/5.6` | Slow linear dolly (`0.25–0.45 m/s`) toward bright aperture |
| **E / H (Misty Lake / Monumental Cliff Vista)** | `70mm – 135mm` | `f/5.6 – f/8.0` | Near-static telephoto compression (`1.00× → 1.03×` breathe) |
| **A / J (Canopy Dome / Top-Down Courtyard)** | `24mm – 35mm` | `f/5.6 – f/8.0` | Slow contemplative upward tilt or vertical crane lift |
